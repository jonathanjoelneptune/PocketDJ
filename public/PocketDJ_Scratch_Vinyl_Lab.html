Performance calibration

Use a mouse or touch. The platter pauses while the hand holds the vinyl and resumes at rest.
Physical rule: At the base pose, the record spins at its normal RPM. During a stroke, the platter follows hand displacement, in either direction. At the endpoint it holds still; on the return stroke it reverses. Once the hand releases at base, normal spin resumes without an angle jump.
