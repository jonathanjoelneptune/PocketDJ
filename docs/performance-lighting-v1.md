# Music-driven PocketDJ room lighting

The room now rests at ~76% dark overlay until `room-atmosphere-playing` turns on. This class follows the actual playback state, not the album selection or login state. The scene wakes in 1.65s, returning to near-dark on pause. It darkens the room art, speakers, DJ and wall-light fixtures, but retains readable transport and marquee layers.

A separate static, non-filtered four-gradient performance wash adds colored spill to the left shelf, right shelf, center stage and floor. It fades on as the room wakes; color values are already interpolated over five seconds from each album. The existing floor/DJ/speaker layer hierarchy remains unchanged.

Album sampling now ranks hue bins across an album cover and selects two substantially separated hues when artwork supports them. For a monochromatic cover the second hue is generated by a deterministic rotation of the primary color. RGB crossfading of these endpoint colors still prevents hue-snaps. Default warm/purple anchors have far less effect, increasing diversity across albums. Neutral art uses the existing deterministic fallback.

Room > Music-Driven Scene Lighting controls: paused darkness (0.25–0.92) and album color intensity (0–1.8). Settings persist in `pocketdj-room-performance-lighting-v1`; Reset restores 0.76/1.25. Existing light-bar and moving-spotlight settings are not overwritten. Existing room vibe defaults migrate once only if untouched.

## Regression checks

1. Start the app without playback: room background, DJ, speakers and light bars are dim; the menu and floor playback controls remain readable and clickable.
2. Start a track: the dark veil disappears over 1.65s and the two wall colors plus stage/floor wash appear. Pause: the veil returns and the light bars retain their soft hardware glow.
3. Play different album palettes (red, blue, green, monochrome): witness strong colors and five-second interpolated shifts without a single-frame snap or jumping to amber/purple.
4. Check demo and Spotify playback states, normal desktop, Spotify embedded portrait/landscape phone/tablet, tall ceiling reveal and reduced-motion.
5. Confirm moving spotlights obey their existing desktop-only performance conditions, bar geometry stays the same, scene dimming never captures clicks, and saved Room utilities survive reload.
