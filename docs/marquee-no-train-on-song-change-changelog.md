# Pocket DJ Marquee No Train on Song Change Changelog

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Changed:</span> When changing songs with next/previous track, the outgoing title is reset to the single original title before the transition begins.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Changed:</span> The incoming title enters as a single original title first. The duplicated `TITLE ✦ TITLE` scroll train is only created after the entry transition finishes.

<span style="color:#dc2626;">Removed:</span> Visible `TITLE ✦ TITLE` train during song-change transitions.
