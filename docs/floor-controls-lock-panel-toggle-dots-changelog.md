# Pocket DJ Floor Controls Lock, Panel Toggle, and Dots Changelog

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Added:</span> A dim lock button fades in next to the `Controls` button when the floor controls are visible.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Added:</span> Clicking the lock button keeps the floor playback controls open and disables the 10-second auto-hide.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Changed:</span> Clicking the `...` floor button now toggles the right utility panel open or closed.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Changed:</span> The `...` dots scale larger so they visually fill the button better at fullscreen.

<span style="color:#dc2626;">Removed:</span> Any remaining floor-control pulse animation while playing or locked.
