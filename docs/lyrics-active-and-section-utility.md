# Pocket DJ Active Lyric and Section Utility

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Added:</span> Active lyric X position, Y position, width, height, zoom, black stroke, background opacity, and background color utilities.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Changed:</span> Replaced the old top/middle/bottom inactive lyric guides with separate past and future lyric sections.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Added:</span> Past lyrics section controls: top guide X/Y/width and bottom guide X/Y/width.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Added:</span> Future lyrics section controls: top guide X/Y/width and bottom guide X/Y/width.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Changed:</span> The active lyric remains independent between the past and future sections.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Changed:</span> Future lyrics fade in within the future section, the active lyric animates separately, and past lyrics fade into the past section after leaving active state.

<span style="color:#16a34a;">Changed:</span> Room utility storage key bumped to `pocketdj-room-utility-v4` so stale lyric geometry from older versions does not override the new section defaults.
